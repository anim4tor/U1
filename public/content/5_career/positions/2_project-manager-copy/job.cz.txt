Details: <p>Our company supplies interiors for office and commercial premises. Our main clients are IBM, Avast, Kentico, Miele, Konica-Minolta and others, we create their new interiors for them. We work with major architects and developers across the country.&nbsp;</p><p>Coordination and planning sets things in motion for us.</p><p>Anyone who wants to take on a&nbsp;project and see it through to completion will be a&nbsp;valuable colleague for us. The implementation of office interiors takes place in the environment of technologically advanced buildings and today's&nbsp;offices resemble more of a&nbsp;quality space to live in. &nbsp;We manage everything during the implementation. From working with the client and team to the contractor. We influence budgets, quality, and most importantly all the implementation processes.</p><p>The level of education is not essential to us, but a&nbsp;talent for planning, forecasting, and managing construction resources will be a&nbsp;great asset in the toolkit of our future project manager. We are prepared to teach you our craft from scratch, however, with certain prerequisites this will not be possible.</p><p>We are technically oriented, but we manage all relationships at a&nbsp;very good communicative level. The result is then a&nbsp;successful implementation day, and that's&nbsp;worth it.</p>
